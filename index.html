Features

Everything at a glance.

Designed for a phone resting on its charger: big, bold type, deep blacks and nothing that gets in your way.

🕰️

13 pages, 10 clock styles

Swipe sideways through widget pages. On the Clock page, swipe up or down to switch between Digital, Analog, Solar, Float, Flip, Glow, Word Clock, Dot Matrix, Duotone and Ring.

↻

Portrait & landscape

Every view has its own layout for each orientation, so it looks right however you dock the phone.

☁︎

Live weather

Current conditions, highs and lows, and an hourly forecast from Open‑Meteo. No API key or account needed.

♫

Now Playing

Album art, progress and play, pause and skip controls for Spotify, YouTube Music and other players.

☾

Night Mode

A red, sleep‑friendly tint that you can set to Off, Auto (22:00–06:00) or On.

◎

Day Progress & Batteries

Activity‑style rings for how much of the day, week, month and year has passed, plus a large battery ring.

✦

Thoughtful details

Burn‑in protection, keep‑screen‑on, 12/24‑hour time, °C/°F, a smooth sweeping second hand, a reduced‑motion option and glass controls.

Charging animation

Plug in. Watch it power up.

When you connect a charger, So StandBy jumps straight to the Batteries stand and plays a full‑screen animation. Afterwards a ‹ Back button appears for 3 seconds and takes you back to the page you were on.

USB‑C cable

Energy from the port

A stream of particles flies in from the edge where the USB‑C port is. The app reads the screen rotation, so the port is at the bottom in portrait and on the left or right in landscape.

Wireless

A ring from the center

A shimmering, MagSafe‑style ring blooms from the middle of the screen, then settles into the battery ring and fills to your charge level.

Get started

Up and running in a minute.

Download the APK

Get the latest release from GitHub. It's free, with no ads and no sign‑up.

Install & allow

Open the file and allow installing from this source. On first launch, grant Location for weather and Notification access for media.

Dock & enjoy

Put your phone on its charger. Swipe to change views, tap to show the controls, and long‑press to open Settings.
