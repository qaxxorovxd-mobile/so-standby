How do I install it?

Download the APK from GitHub Releases, open the file on your Android phone, and allow installation from that source if Android asks. Open So StandBy, choose your view, and rest your phone on a stand.

Which permissions do I need?

Location lets the app show local weather. You can also select a city in Settings. Notification access enables the Now Playing view to read and control your active media session. You can use the clock without either permission.

How do I change the view?

Swipe left or right to change pages. On the Clock page, swipe up or down to try another style. Tap to show controls, or long-press to open Settings and choose your accent color.
